02 · Deliverables

Everything included.

Not a stripped-down version of something bigger. Everything you need to launch a modern website that works on every device, ranks on Google, and converts visitors into clients.

01 / Design

Custom design.

A custom design that fits your brand and industry — not a recycled template. Editorial typography, intentional layouts, considered colour. Every screen designed before we touch the code.

02 / Build

Modern build.

Hand-coded, mobile-first, optimised for Core Web Vitals. PageSpeed scores typically 90+. No bloated WordPress themes — we use lean, fast, modern frameworks.

03 / SEO

SEO foundation.

Keyword research, optimised title tags and meta descriptions on every page, structured data, sitemap, Google Search Console setup. Built so you can rank from day one.

04 / CMS

A CMS you can use.

Edit text and images yourself, no developer required. Visual editing — what you see is what your visitors see. A 15-minute training video to get you confident.

05 / Analytics

Analytics setup.

Google Analytics 4 configured properly. Conversion tracking on contact forms and key CTAs. Monthly traffic snapshot so you can actually see what's working.

06 / Forms

Smart forms.

Spam-protected contact form that sends to your inbox reliably. Multi-step where it makes sense, single-step where it doesn't. With booking integration if you want it.

07 / Content

Content polish.

We rewrite weak headlines and copy where needed. You keep your voice — we just sharpen it so visitors immediately understand what you do and why they should care.

08 / Local SEO

Local presence.

Google Business Profile linked, local schema markup, city-specific landing pages where relevant. Designed to help you rank for "near me" searches in your target city.

09 / Launch

Launch support.

SSL, hosting setup, DNS migration, 301 redirects from old URLs. We handle the technical handover so nothing breaks the day you go live.

05 · Is this right for you?

Full Rebuild is for serious businesses.

A good fit if

  • Your current site no longer reflects your business
  • You're losing leads or clients to competitors with better sites
  • You want to rank for specific keywords on Google
  • You need a CMS so you can edit content yourself
  • You're rebranding or repositioning
  • You're scaling and need a website that scales with you
  • You want to look credible to bigger clients

Not a fit if

  • Your current site mostly works and just needs a refresh
  • You need ongoing SEO + content marketing afterwards
  • You're building an e-commerce store with 100+ products
