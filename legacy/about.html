About Folio

A small, opinionated
web agency from Luxembourg.

We build websites for ambitious SMEs — Luxembourg, London, Amsterdam, New York, Paris, Singapore, Dubai. Most agencies overcomplicate it. We don't.

Founded in 2024 with one rule: every site we ship should be faster, sharper, and more profitable than the one it replaces. No long discovery phases. No bloated retainers. No theatre — just sites that work, delivered when we said they'd be delivered.

How we work

Six things we
do differently.

The principles behind how we run projects. They're why we can deliver in seven days while most agencies need seven weeks.

01 / Speed is the feature

Seven days, always.

Most agencies treat timelines as suggestions. We treat them as commitments. Quick Refresh ships in 5 days. Full Rebuild ships in 10. Growth Partner kicks off within 2 weeks. We hit those numbers because the alternative is unacceptable.

02 / Pricing is fixed

No surprise invoices.

Every package has a fixed price range, posted publicly on the services page. We confirm the exact number before any work starts. Scope changes get quoted in writing before they happen. You'll never get a bill you didn't expect.

03 / Audit before action

The audit is free.

We won't quote you without seeing your site first. Every prospect gets a free 12-metric audit and a PDF report — even if they never become a client. It's the only honest way to recommend a package.

04 / Design then code

Every screen, designed.

No "wireframe and iterate in code" nonsense. Every page is designed in Figma to pixel completion before we touch the codebase. You see and approve direction before we commit to building. Saves you days, saves us weeks.

05 / Lean tech

Modern stack. No bloat.

Astro, Next.js, Sanity, Vercel, Cloudflare. We don't use WordPress unless you explicitly need it. Our sites typically score 95+ on PageSpeed out of the box — because they're built lean, not retrofitted lean.

06 / Measure everything

Numbers, not vibes.

Every site we launch has analytics + conversion tracking from day one. We report on real numbers — traffic, rankings, enquiries — not "the site looks great." If a change doesn't improve a metric, we change it back.

The story

Why we
started.

After a decade across product design and engineering — at agencies, in-house teams, and on freelance contracts — we kept seeing the same thing: small businesses being charged €15–30k for websites that should cost a third of that, delivered in a quarter of the time.

The pattern was the same everywhere. Long discovery phases dressing up a basic brief. Bloated retainers padding the invoice. Endless revisions because nothing was actually designed before the build started. By the time the site launched, the client had spent six months and forgotten why they started.

Most agencies sell complexity. We sell the opposite: speed, predictability, and a finished site you can be proud of.

Folio was started to do this properly. Three principles: fixed pricing, fixed timelines, design before code. No exceptions. We pick a small number of projects each month, deliver them on time, and move on. The clients we love are the ones who don't want to hand-hold an agency for a year — they want to launch, see results, and get back to running their business.

Based in Luxembourg. Working with clients across Europe, the UK, the US, and Asia. Small team, sharp focus.
