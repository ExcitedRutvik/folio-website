# Trayaam SEO Audit: Technical and On-Page

Branch: `feature/content-review-seo` · Date: 2026-06-28 · Scope: every route (47 URLs), code plus live `trayaam.com`.

## Data caveat
GA4 was not authenticated at audit time (MCP connected, no credentials) and there is no Search Console connection, so this audit is based on the codebase plus live crawling (curl, built `dist`, response headers). Performance could not be measured with Lighthouse locally (no headless Chrome). Numbers from GA4 and GSC, plus a PageSpeed Insights run, should be layered in next.

## Executive summary
The site is technically well built and unusually strong on structured data and answer-engine readiness for a small agency. The foundation (canonical tags, OG and Twitter cards, Organization, WebSite, LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Article, Service and ContactPage schema, sitemap, `llms.txt`, GA4, a 19-post question-led blog) is already in place.

Three things hold it back, in order of importance:

1. **AI crawlers are blocked at the edge**, which directly contradicts the AEO and GEO positioning the whole site is built around.
2. **The positioning is wrong.** Titles, copy and structure sell "web design and 7-day rebuilds", but Trayaam is a full funnel digital transformation and growth partner. Half the services have no page at all. (Strategy detail lives in `seo/keyword-research.md`.)
3. **A set of concrete technical defects** (self-canonical points to a redirect, OG image is an SVG, invalid Article dates, oversized meta descriptions) that are individually small and collectively drag the site.

Plus one conversion blocker that is not strictly SEO but wastes every click that SEO would earn: the contact form is a non-functional demo.

## 1. Technical SEO

### T1. AI crawlers blocked at the edge (HIGH, strategic)
Live `robots.txt` is not the repo file. Cloudflare injects a "Managed Content" block ahead of it:
```
User-agent: * Content-Signal: search=yes,ai-train=no
User-agent: ClaudeBot      Disallow: /
User-agent: GPTBot         Disallow: /
User-agent: Google-Extended Disallow: /
(plus Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended, Bytespider, CCBot, meta-externalagent)
```
The repo `public/robots.txt` then appends `Allow` rules for the same bots, creating a direct conflict. Net effect: GPTBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended are told to stay out, which is exactly the opposite of the AEO and GEO strategy the site advertises (the homepage terminal even prints "Optimising semantic SEO", the blog teaches "get cited by ChatGPT", and the site ships `llms.txt`).
**Evidence:** `curl https://trayaam.com/robots.txt`.
**Fix:** in the Cloudflare dashboard, turn off the managed AI-bot block (AI Audit / "block AI crawlers") or set content signals to permit `ai-input`, so GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended can crawl. Then reconcile the duplicate groups so the served file is intentional.

### T2. Self-canonical points to a redirecting URL (MEDIUM)
Built pages set canonical and `og:url` without a trailing slash, but those URLs 308-redirect to the trailing-slash version that the sitemap and the live site actually use.
**Evidence:** `dist/about/index.html` canonical is `https://trayaam.com/about`; `curl -I https://trayaam.com/about` returns `308 -> /about/`; sitemap lists `https://trayaam.com/about/`.
**Cause:** `Astro.url.pathname` in `src/layouts/Layout.astro:16`, with no `trailingSlash` set in `astro.config.mjs`.
**Fix:** set `trailingSlash: 'always'` (and keep `build.format: 'directory'`) and ensure the canonical is built with the trailing slash, so canonical, og:url, sitemap and the served URL all agree.

### T3. Open Graph image is an SVG (HIGH, low effort)
`og:image` and `twitter:image` point to `/assets/og-image.svg`. Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X and most AI link unfurlers do not render SVG, so shared links show no preview image.
**Evidence:** `src/layouts/Layout.astro:11,47`; confirmed in `dist/about/index.html`.
**Fix:** export a 1200x630 PNG or JPG, set it as the default `ogImage`, and add `og:image:width` and `og:image:height`.

### T4. Article schema has invalid dates (MEDIUM)
Blog posts pass human strings such as "Feb 2026" straight into `datePublished`, which expects ISO 8601. `dateModified`, `image` and a real author are missing.
**Evidence:** `src/layouts/Post.astro:7-8`, dates from `src/data/blog.js`.
**Fix:** store an ISO date per post (`2026-02-01`), keep the display string separate, add `dateModified` and `image`, and use a Person or Organization author consistently.

### T5. Duplicate geo meta (LOW)
`geo.region` and `geo.placename` are each declared twice (London then Ahmedabad); the second overwrites the first, so the London signal is lost.
**Evidence:** `src/layouts/Layout.astro:56-59`.
**Fix:** geo meta only holds one value. Drop these and rely on the two PostalAddress entries in LocalBusiness schema, which already handle both cities.

### T6. No BreadcrumbList schema (MEDIUM)
Nested URLs (`/services/*`, `/industries/*`, `/work/*`, `/blog/*`) have no breadcrumb markup, so the breadcrumb rich result and the extra crawl context are missed.
**Fix:** add a `BreadcrumbList` to the nested page layouts.

### T7. Sitemap has no lastmod (LOW)
`dist/sitemap-0.xml` contains zero `lastmod` entries, so search engines get no freshness signal.
**Fix:** feed real modified dates into the sitemap (date data on posts plus a serialize step in `@astrojs/sitemap`).

### T8. NAP weak for UK (MEDIUM)
The only phone number across schema and the contact page is the +91 India line, while the brand leads with London. There is one LocalBusiness covering two studios.
**Evidence:** `src/layouts/Layout.astro:80`, `src/pages/contact.astro`, `src/pages/index.astro`.
**Fix:** add a UK phone number, and consider two LocalBusiness entries (one per studio) so each city has consistent NAP for local search.

### T9. areaServed is bloated for the chosen scope (LOW, strategy)
Schema and `llms.txt` list New York, Amsterdam, Paris, Singapore and Dubai. With scope now set to UK and India only, this dilutes local relevance and misrepresents focus.
**Evidence:** `src/layouts/Layout.astro:75`, `src/pages/index.astro` LocalBusiness, `public/llms.txt`.
**Fix:** trim `areaServed` to UK and India (London, Ahmedabad as anchors).

### T10. Missing security headers (LOW)
Live responses include `x-content-type-options` and `referrer-policy` but no HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options or Permissions-Policy. Best-practice and a minor trust and "best practices" Lighthouse factor.
**Fix:** add the headers at Cloudflare or the host.

### T11. HTML is not edge-cached (LOW, performance)
`cf-cache-status: DYNAMIC` and `cache-control: max-age=0, must-revalidate` mean the static HTML is fetched fresh each time rather than served from Cloudflare's edge, costing TTFB.
**Fix:** allow Cloudflare to cache HTML for a static site (cache rule or appropriate cache-control), with normal purging on deploy.

### T12. OG and Twitter completeness (INFO)
Missing `og:site_name`, `article:published_time` on posts, `og:image:width/height`, and `twitter:site`.

### T13. Thin E-E-A-T and page-type schema (LOW)
`/about` has no Person schema for the three named team members (no roles, no `sameAs` social profiles). `/tech`, `/work`, `/industries` and `/sample-audit` carry only the global Organization graph, no page-type schema.
**Fix:** add Person entries (with `sameAs` to LinkedIn etc.), and page-appropriate schema (AboutPage, CollectionPage or ItemList for listings, CreativeWork for case studies).

### T14. Performance (likely strong, unverified)
Architecture is excellent for Core Web Vitals: static Astro output, minimal inline JS, lazy-loaded images with width and height, font preconnect and preload, edge hosting. The site claims 95 to 100 Lighthouse and that is plausible. Could not verify locally (no Chrome). One small risk: the Google Fonts stylesheet is render-blocking. Confirm real LCP, INP and CLS via PageSpeed Insights and GSC field data.

### T15. Internal linking (good)
Every internal page link resolves; no broken links were found across pages and blog bodies (checked programmatically against the route set). `/sample-audit` is linked from the footer, so it is not orphaned. Minor gap: the main nav links to `/services` but not to individual service pages, so the new silo pages will need a nav or mega-menu plan.

## 2. On-page SEO (page by page)

H1s are clean: exactly one per page (the index "2" is CSS class reuse on a CTA `h2`, not a second H1; legacy blog H1s render from the Post layout). The recurring problems are framing and meta-description length.

| Route | Title issue | Desc length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | Web-design framing ("Editorial Websites") | 172 | Reposition to outcome; H1 is brand-led, low keyword |
| `/about` | OK | 186 (long) | No Person schema; trim desc |
| `/services` | "Web Design Services & Pricing" | 153 | Should be the full-funnel hub, not a 3-package menu |
| `/services/full-rebuild` | OK | 165 (long) | Reframe as a package under web build |
| `/services/quick-refresh` | OK | within range | Reframe as package |
| `/services/growth` | "SEO + Website" buries SEO | 161 (long) | SEO deserves its own page |
| `/services/ai-automation` | OK | 183 (long) | Expand to sales and business-process automation; trim desc |
| `/work` and 4 case studies | OK | 176 on listing | Add CreativeWork and Breadcrumb schema |
| `/industries` | OK | 200 (too long) | Trim; reframe from "web design for X" to full funnel |
| `/industries/[industry]` | OK | Dynamic, far too long | Built from intro plus keywords; will truncate badly |
| `/tech` | OK | 164 (long) | Fine, light trim |
| `/contact` | OK | 144 | Form is a demo (see C3) |
| `/sample-audit` | OK | 159 | Strong lead magnet; link it from the new SEO page |
| `/blog` and posts | OK | within range | Strongest section; see content notes |

**Cross-cutting on-page issues**
- O1. Oversized meta descriptions on `/industries` (200), `/about` (186), `/services/ai-automation` (183), `/work` (176), `/` (172), `/services/full-rebuild` (165), `/tech` (164), `/services/growth` (161), and the dynamic industry pages. Target 150 to 160.
- O2. Titles and copy are web-design framed and contradict the full-funnel positioning. This is the largest on-page item and is handled in the rewrite.
- O3. Em dashes are everywhere in titles and body copy (every page title uses one). They must all be removed per the writing rule.
- O4. H1s are brand-clever but keyword-light. Keep the voice but make sure the primary term appears in the H1 or the first H2 and opening sentence.
- O5. Missing service pages (Content, UX/UI, standalone SEO, Social, GMB, Performance, broader automation). Half the offer is invisible to search. See `seo/keyword-research.md`.

## 3. Positioning and keyword alignment
Covered in full in `seo/keyword-research.md`. Summary: the site needs to move from a web-design studio frame to a "Digital Transformation and Growth Partner" frame, with a two-layer keyword model (demand or umbrella, plus capture silos), UK and India only, city anchor plus nationwide. Every title, H1, meta and the schema `description` fields should carry the new frame.

## 4. Content and AEO (the strong suit)
The blog is the best SEO asset on the site: 19 question-led posts in three clusters, sensible internal linking, FAQ schema on the homepage and industry pages, and an `llms.txt`. Keep this engine and extend it to the new silos (for example "how much does PPC cost for a small business", "what is business process automation", "do I need SEO or Google Ads"). The one thing actively undermining it is T1, the edge-level AI-bot block.

## 5. Conversion blocker (not SEO, but flagged)
C3. The contact form is a front-end demo that never submits ("wire to your inbox/Resend before launch", `src/pages/contact.astro:105`). Every CTA on every page funnels here. Any SEO traffic gained is wasted until the form actually delivers enquiries. Wire it to email or Resend before or alongside the SEO push.
