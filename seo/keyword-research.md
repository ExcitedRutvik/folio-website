# Trayaam Keyword Research and Strategy

Branch: `feature/content-review-seo` · Last updated: 2026-06-28

## How to read this
Trayaam is a full funnel digital agency that sells an outcome ("digital transformation and growth"), not a service menu. So the keyword plan runs on two layers:

- **Layer 1, Demand / Umbrella.** What the business *is* and the need a client feels. This is the hero positioning and the top of the funnel. Lower direct purchase intent, higher volume, sets the brand frame.
- **Layer 2, Capture / Service silos.** The named services people search when they are ready to buy. Each silo gets its own page, tuned to its head term, and links back up to the umbrella.

Both feed each other: need searchers enter through the hero and blog, ready to buy searchers enter through a silo page and climb to the umbrella.

Every keyword below is tagged with intent, a target page, a geo set, and a priority (P0 highest, then P1, P2). Search volumes are not filled in yet because GA4 and Search Console were not authenticated at the time of writing (the GA4 MCP is connected but has no credentials). Once GSC query data is available, this doc gets a volume and "already ranking / striking distance" column.

## Markets and geo
UK and India only. US is dropped. Keep British spelling, GBP pricing, `en_GB`.

Geo model is **city anchor plus nationwide**:
- UK: London (anchor) and UK wide.
- India: Ahmedabad (anchor) and India wide.

Geo suffix reference applied across money terms below: `London`, `UK`, `Ahmedabad`, `India`.

## Bifurcation key (as requested)
- **Primary / head:** short, high value money terms. One dedicated page each.
- **Secondary / supporting:** mid intent terms that reinforce a primary page.
- **Long tail:** lower competition, higher conversion, often geo or qualifier modified.
- **Question / AEO:** answer engine and "people also ask" style queries. Mostly blog.

# Industry layer (already strong, keep and extend)
`src/data/industries.js` already carries explicit keywords per industry (architecture, hospitality, professional services, manufacturing, retail, SaaS, real estate, healthcare, legal, construction). These are good long tail capture pages. Action: add geo modified variants (for example `restaurant website design Ahmedabad`, `law firm website design UK`) and reframe each subpage so the service it points to is the new silo, not just "web design".

# Question / AEO layer (existing blog, keep and grow)
The blog already targets 19 question style queries (cost, timelines, WordPress, builders, ranking, AI citation, enquiries). This is the single strongest SEO asset on the site. Action: extend it toward the new silos that have no question content yet, for example: "how much does social media management cost", "what is business process automation", "do I need Google Ads or SEO", "how much does PPC cost for a small business".

# Location pages (new)
- `/web-design-london`, `/digital-marketing-london` style anchors for UK.
- `/web-design-ahmedabad`, `/digital-marketing-ahmedabad` style anchors for India.
Geo only lives in meta and schema today, not in indexable page content, which is why head "service plus city" terms are weak. Location pages close that gap.
