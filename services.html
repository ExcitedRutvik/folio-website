Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Still on the fence? These are the things most people ask before getting started.

Can you really deliver a full website in seven days? +

Yes — for sites up to 8 pages. We hit this timeline by working from a tight brief, sending one structured draft by day 3, and keeping revisions to two rounds. We've done this 32 times this year. The pace works because we don't reinvent the wheel: we use proven design patterns, our own component library, and modern tooling.

What happens if I don't like the first draft? +

You get two revision rounds with every package. If the direction is genuinely off after the first round, we'll restart that section at no cost — we'd rather rebuild it than ship something you don't love. That said, this has happened twice in two years.

Do you work with clients outside Luxembourg? +

Yes. We work with SMEs in London, Amsterdam, New York, Paris, Singapore and Dubai. Everything is remote-first — we run discovery calls over video and ship just as fast for international clients as we do locally.

Will my site still rank well on Google? +

Better than before, in almost every case. The Full Rebuild includes a complete SEO foundation: keyword research, proper title tags and meta descriptions on every page, structured data, fast load times, mobile-first build. Most of our clients see ranking improvements within 4–6 weeks of launch.

What if I need ongoing changes after launch? +

Every package includes free post-launch support (2 weeks to 3 months depending on tier). After that, we offer hourly support at €90/hour or monthly retainers from €500. The CMS lets you edit text and images yourself without needing us.

Do you charge upfront? +

50% to start, 50% on launch. For Growth Partner packages, we split it monthly across the engagement. No hidden fees, no surprise invoices.
