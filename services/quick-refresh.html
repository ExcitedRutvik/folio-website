04 · Is this right for you?

Quick Refresh isn't for everyone.

It's the right call if your existing site has good bones — content, structure, brand — but feels old. It's the wrong call if you're starting from zero or want a fundamentally different site.

A good fit if

  • Your site is 3–7 years old and looks like it
  • You're happy with your existing content and structure
  • You want it fast and don't need a complete redesign
  • You have a tight budget but still need to look credible
  • You're keeping the same domain and CMS
  • Most of your problems are speed, mobile, or SEO

Not a fit if

  • You want to rebrand or change visual identity
  • You need more than 3 pages designed
  • You're moving from an old platform to a new one
  • You want a CMS to edit pages yourself
  • You need ongoing SEO or marketing support
  • You want a complete content rewrite

If Quick Refresh isn't right, Full Rebuild probably is.
