04 · Quarterly deliverables

Every 90 days,
we go deeper.

Q01 · Competitor map

Who's beating you.

Deep audit of 3–5 competitors in your city. Where they rank, what content they publish, what backlinks they have, what their site does better than yours. With an action plan to close the gap.

Q02 · Conversion deep-dive

Why visitors leave.

Session recordings, heatmaps, funnel analysis. We watch how real visitors interact with your site, find the friction points, and ship fixes. Typically lifts conversion rate by 15–30%.

Q03 · Content audit

What to refresh.

Page-by-page review of every piece of content. Which pages drive traffic, which need rewriting, which should be removed. Refresh plan for the next quarter, with priorities.
