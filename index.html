06 · Questions

Things people ask
before they start.

Honest answers. If yours isn't here, email us at hello@folio.lu — real human replies within one working day.

Ask us directly
How is 7 days actually possible? +

We audit first so there's no guessing phase. Design and development run concurrently. We use a proven stack (Astro / Next.js / Netlify) we've deployed dozens of times, not custom experiments. Two structured review rounds — not open-ended feedback loops. It's not a shortcut; it's a system we've refined over 32 projects.

What if I don't like the first draft? +

Every package includes structured revision rounds — Quick Refresh gets one, Full Rebuild and Growth Partner get two. We share a design in Figma before we write a line of code, so changes are cheap and fast at that stage. We've never had a project go to three rounds.

Do you work with clients outside Luxembourg? +

Yes — we work fully remote with clients in London, Amsterdam, New York, Paris, Singapore and Dubai. The audit and discovery call happen over video; everything else is async. Time zone difference hasn't been an issue for any project to date.

Will my site actually rank on Google? +

We can't guarantee rankings — nobody honestly can. What we can guarantee is an SEO foundation that gives you the best possible starting position: correct title tags, schema markup, sitemap, fast load times, and mobile-first structure. Most clients see meaningful movement within 6–8 weeks of launch.

What happens after launch? +

Full Rebuild and Growth Partner include 30 days of free post-launch support. Growth Partner adds ongoing monthly SEO, reports, and competitor analysis. Quick Refresh clients get 7 days of support. Beyond that, we're available on a retained basis — ask us about monthly maintenance.

Do you charge upfront? +

50% on project kick-off, 50% on launch day. One price, in writing, before we start — zero scope creep, zero surprise invoices. The free audit is always genuinely free: no card required, no hidden strings.
